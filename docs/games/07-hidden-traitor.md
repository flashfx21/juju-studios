# Hidden traitor game (working title)

**Game ID:** `hidden-traitor`  
**Status:** Phase 3; marquee candidate (needs its own design pass and playtesting)  
**Players:** 4 to 10 (placeholder)  
**Length:** 30 min (placeholder)  
**Modes:** Hidden roles

## Pitch
Grow the prize pot together while secret traitors sabotage. Banish them before they win.

## Decided
- Setup: phones secretly deal roles. 1 traitor for 4 to 6 players, 2 for 7 to 10; traitors see each other
- Mission: a quick group challenge grows a shared pot; answers are private; the TV shows only the total; traitors can quietly answer wrong
- Roundtable: argue out loud, then vote privately to banish one player; their role is revealed
- Night: everyone looks at their phone at once; traitors pick a player to eliminate; everyone else gets a decoy task
- Winning: faithful banish every traitor; traitors win by equaling the faithful or surviving to the final round; winners split the pot as leaderboard points
- Family rule: eliminated players keep playing missions as ghosts but can't vote
- Missions reuse rounds from other suite games
