# Curated board game (name open)

**Game ID:** `board-game`  
**Status:** Phase 3 (deep dive; biggest scope item)  
**Players:** 2 to 8 (placeholder)  
**Length:** 45 min (placeholder)  
**Modes:** Individual

## Pitch
Race from start to finish with life choices, careers and events along the way.

## Decided
- A start-to-finish path like Candy Land, Chutes and Ladders or Sorry, with Game of Life-style depth: choices, careers or paths, money or resources, life events
- TV is the board; phones are each player's hand, spinner and choices

## Open questions
- Theme and what the life choices are
- Luck vs. decisions; target length
- Can players affect each other (like bumping in Sorry)?
- Save and resume a game?

## Engine pieces it needs
- Board on TV, spinner, choices, longer game saves

## Content pack
Board layout, events, careers

## Scoring
Reports a finishing order only. The engine applies the suite rules: 100 points per game; top 50% score on a countdown split; 2 teams 80/20; 3+ teams top 2 score (67/33); team points split evenly.

## Naming and IP
Inspired by classic family board games. Original board, names and art.
