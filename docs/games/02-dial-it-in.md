# Dial It In

**Game ID:** `dial-it-in`  
**Status:** Development. Launch phase 1. Rules, phone screen and guess dial (stops at both ends) done; solo playtest live; rooms on phones live (Nov 14): people take turns giving clues, bots only guess, private targets and guesses, host Next round. TV screen and Instructions to do  
**Players:** 3 to 10 (placeholder)  
**Length:** 20 min (placeholder)  
**Modes:** Individual (team average is an open option)

## Pitch
A hidden target sits on a spectrum between two opposites. The clue giver gives a clue; everyone guesses privately where it lands.

## Decided
- Everyone guesses privately on their own phone, so the loudest person doesn't decide
- Number entry is the base input
- Range by agreed difficulty: 1 to 50 or 1 to 100
- Experiment: a dial on top of number entry, especially for touch screens

## Open questions
- Score each player by closeness, or average into one team guess?
- Which input is the default: dial or number?
- Clue giver rotation

## Engine pieces it needs
- Private number input, optional dial control, turn order

## Content pack
Spectrum card pack (pairs of opposites)

## Scoring
Reports a finishing order only. The engine applies the suite rules: 100 points per game; top 50% score on a countdown split; 2 teams 80/20; 3+ teams top 2 score (67/33); team points split evenly.

## Naming and IP
Inspired by Wavelength. Original name and spectrum list before anything goes public.
