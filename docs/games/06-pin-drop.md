# Pin Drop (working title)

**Game ID:** `pin-drop`  
**Status:** Playable, phase 1  
**Players:** 2 to 10 (placeholder)  
**Length:** 15 min (placeholder)  
**Modes:** Individual

## Pitch
A photo appears on the TV. Everyone drops a pin on a map on their phone. Closest wins.

## Decided
- Must use photos (no text-only clues)
- Photo sources to explore: Wikimedia Commons (free, openly licensed, many with locations; show credit) and Mapillary (free street-level photos; needs a free API token; confirm terms)
- Later: family photo packs from your own trips (strip location data from anything public)
- Avoid for now: Google Street View (needs billing)
- Map on the phone: OpenStreetMap-based (e.g. Leaflet), free with attribution

## Open questions
- Wikimedia or Mapillary first?
- Scoring by distance: bands or a curve?
- Rounds per game

## Engine pieces it needs
- Map on phone, photo packs with credits

## Content pack
Photo pack: image, latitude, longitude, credit, difficulty

## Scoring
Reports a finishing order only. The engine applies the suite rules: 100 points per game; top 50% score on a countdown split; 2 teams 80/20; 3+ teams top 2 score (67/33); team points split evenly.

## Naming and IP
Photos need licenses or credits unless they're your own.
