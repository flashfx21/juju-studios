# Chainword (working name)

**Game ID:** `chainword`  
**Status:** Playable, phase 2  
**Players:** 2 to 10 (placeholder)  
**Length:** 10 min (placeholder)  
**Modes:** Individual race

## Pitch
Race through a chain of hidden words in hard mode. Fewest guesses wins.

## Decided
- Win condition: fewest guesses
- A chain of 3 or 5 words per round; same chain for everyone
- Hard mode enforced: revealed hints must be used in later guesses (yellow letters reused)
- Everyone guesses on their own phone; the TV shows each player's progress

## Open questions
- Name: Chainword is the strongest so far; keep looking
- Hard mode scope: yellow only, or also keep greens in place?
- Chain length fixed or a host setting?
- Guess cap per word, penalty for a missed word, tiebreaker (total time?)
- Does the TV show other players' colored squares (no letters) as pressure?

## Engine pieces it needs
- Private text input, per-player progress on TV

## Content pack
Word list (original, curated)

## Scoring
Reports a finishing order only. The engine applies the suite rules: 100 points per game; top 50% score on a countdown split; 2 teams 80/20; 3+ teams top 2 score (67/33); team points split evenly.
