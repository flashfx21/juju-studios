# Sketch Showdown

**Game ID:** `sketch-showdown`  
**Status:** Playable, phase 3  
**Players:** 4 to 10 (placeholder)  
**Length:** 15 min (placeholder)  
**Modes:** Teams

## Pitch
Two teams, one clue, two drawers. First team to guess wins the round.

## Decided
- Two teams; one drawer each; both sketch the same clue at the same time
- Teammates guess on their phones; first team to guess wins the round
- Once guessed, both drawings are shown to the whole lobby

## Open questions
- Can each team see the other team's sketch while drawing? (If yes, teams can copy guesses.)
- Drawer rotation
- Points per round, timer, and what happens if nobody guesses

## Engine pieces it needs
- Teams, live drawing sent from phone to TV

## Content pack
Clue list

## Scoring
Reports a finishing order only. The engine applies the suite rules: 100 points per game; top 50% score on a countdown split; 2 teams 80/20; 3+ teams top 2 score (67/33); team points split evenly.
