## Beyond the rule

- **Phrase categories are too loose.** DB-026 Things you do on a snow day, DB-030 Bad first-date moves, DB-034 Signs you're getting old, DB-035 Things said in a work meeting, DB-036 Reasons to leave a party early and DB-039 Excuses to skip the gym take whole phrases, so any letter works by rewording ("Getting tired", "Taking a nap"). They pass the rule easily, but the letter barely constrains them, and they invite padding challenges.
- **DB-038 Shows to binge overlaps DB-024 TV shows.** Most answers fit both. A night that deals both repeats itself.
